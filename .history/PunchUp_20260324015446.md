# Punch-Up Proposal

## What is the work you're taking on?

I am creating an interactive scene inside my About page

The scene will include:

- A pixel small character version of myself
- A static (3 places on one row, 2 rows)layout with illustrated locations:
    - Coffee shop
    - Headphones
    - Park
    - Desk
    - Painting
    - Rain

Users will be able to:

- Click and drag the character
- Drop the character into one of the locations

When the character enters a location:

- A small animation or state change will occur
- A short description about me will appear? 🤔

---

## How will this enhance your body of work?

This will enhance my portfolio in several ways, it demonstrates:

- Interactive storytelling about myself
- Visitors will discover my hobbies by interacting
- This interaction makes my About page more playful
